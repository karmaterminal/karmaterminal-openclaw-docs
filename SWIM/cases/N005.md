# Case `N005` — Chain-budget anti-flood (fanout consumes 1 chain-step regardless of recipient count)

**Family**: Guards
**Lifecycle status**: new
**Evidence class needed**: live-row
**Provenance**: RFC §6.8 §6.7 mercy-clause; chain-budget anti-flood contract
**Registry version added**: v1

## Claim being proved

Per-completion fan-out consumes exactly 1 chain-step regardless of recipient count. A 50-recipient `fanoutMode: "all"` decrements `chainStepBudgetRemaining` by 1, NOT by 50. Mercy-clause holds: when `chainStepBudgetRemaining <= 0`, traceparent NOT threaded past cap (cuts chain visibility past the budget exhaustion point).

## Required minimum row shape

Build chain to known budget level. Fire fanout with large recipient count (e.g. 50). Verify `chainStepBudgetRemaining` decremented by 1, not by recipient count. Drive budget to 0 and verify mercy-clause: traceparent stops threading past the cap point.

## Evidence surface(s)

`chainStepBudgetRemaining` measurement pre/post-fanout; recipient count vs decrement size; trace-tree query showing traceparent absence past cap.
